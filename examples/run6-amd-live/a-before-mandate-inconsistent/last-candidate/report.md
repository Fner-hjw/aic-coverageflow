## Results

Data Center carries the largest weight in the forecast blend, reflecting its share of the latest trailing revenue base; Client and Gaming is the next-largest contributor, while Embedded has the smallest weight [EV:dcf.blend.weights]. The proposal assumes a flat gross margin of 53.0% through the forecast period [EV:dcf.base.gross_margin].

The valuation build holds the latest trailing operating margin and capital expenditure burden constant as revenue grows, and assumes no incremental working-capital investment [EV:dcf.base.ebit_margin] [EV:dcf.base.capex_pct_revenue]. The forecast should therefore be read as the accepted base case, not as company guidance.
