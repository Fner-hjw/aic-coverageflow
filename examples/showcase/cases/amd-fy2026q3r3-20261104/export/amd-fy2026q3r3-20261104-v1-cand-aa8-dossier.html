Summary

AMD delivered a strong quarter, with revenue of USD 13,377mm, up 44.7% year over year, and operating income of USD 2,832mm, up 123.0%1–4. Gross margin reached 53.9%, compared with 51.7% a year earlier, while diluted EPS increased to USD 1.46 from USD 0.755, 6. Data Center led the advance; Client and Gaming revenue and operating income declined year over year7–9. Our thesis is more dependent on Data Center growth, while the quarter’s increase in inventory and accounts receivable raises execution and cash conversion questions10, 11. The Gordon DCF indicates a target of USD 47.79 per share and a Sell rating; terminal methods diverge materially, so the output warrants caution12–14.

Results

Quarterly revenue rose to USD 13,377mm from USD 9,246mm in the prior-year quarter1. Gross profit increased to USD 7,210mm from USD 4,780mm, and gross margin expanded to 53.9% from 51.7%1, 5, 15. Operating income grew to USD 2,832mm, with operating margin rising to 21.2% from 13.7%2, 16. The improvement reflects revenue growth and higher gross profit, partly offset by higher operating expenses, which increased to USD 4,378mm from USD 3,510mm15, 17.

Net income was USD 2,428mm versus USD 1,243mm, and diluted EPS was USD 1.46 versus USD 0.756, 18. Other income (expense), net was an expense of USD 902mm, compared with income of USD 82mm in the prior-year quarter; interest expense was USD 62mm versus USD 37mm19, 20. The non-operating line partly offset the improvement in operating income. For the nine months ended in Q3, revenue was USD 35,166mm versus USD 24,369mm, while operating income was USD 6,298mm versus USD 1,942mm1, 2.

Operating cash flow for the nine-month period was USD 6,523mm, up from USD 5,109mm21. Cash and cash equivalents at quarter end were USD 7,404mm, and short-term investments were USD 8,372mm21–23. The company reported USD 7,079mm of long-term debt and no current portion of long-term debt; the model’s net-debt measure was negative USD 8,697mm, indicating net cash24–26.

Segment detail

Data Center revenue increased to USD 8,546mm from USD 4,341mm, and segment operating income rose to USD 2,882mm from USD 1,074mm7, 27. The operating margin increased to 33.7% from 24.7%7, 27, 28. Data Center generated the clear majority of quarterly revenue and operating profit growth. Nine-month revenue reached USD 21,039mm, compared with USD 11,255mm, while operating income was USD 6,584mm versus USD 1,851mm7, 27.

Client and Gaming revenue declined to USD 3,722mm from USD 4,048mm8. Segment operating income fell to USD 573mm from USD 867mm, and operating margin narrowed to 15.4% from 21.4%8, 9, 29. The nine-month comparison was firmer: revenue increased to USD 11,168mm from USD 10,610mm, though operating income was USD 1,730mm versus USD 2,130mm8, 9.

Embedded revenue advanced to USD 1,109mm from USD 857mm30. Operating income was USD 453mm versus USD 283mm, with margin of 40.8% compared with 33.0%30–32. Nine-month revenue grew to USD 2,959mm from USD 2,504mm, and operating income rose to USD 1,177mm from USD 886mm30, 31. All Other recorded an operating loss of USD 1,076mm, wider than the USD 954mm loss in the prior-year quarter33.

Outlook

Our forecast path assumes that Data Center remains the principal growth contributor, with growth moderating over time. That direction is consistent with the step-up in Data Center quarterly and nine-month revenue, while the prior-year comparison is increasingly demanding7. The path assumes a more measured contribution from Client and Gaming after its quarterly revenue decline and lower segment operating income8, 9. Embedded is modeled as a gradual recovery, supported by the quarterly and nine-month increases in revenue and operating income, while recognizing its history of lower annual revenue30, 31.

The valuation uses FY2025 as its base because the supplied nine-month cash-flow history does not provide the capital-expenditure inputs needed to construct a trailing twelve-month base. The historical base was revenue of USD 34,639mm, EBITDA of USD 4,444mm, and free cash flow of USD 6,697mm34–37. Those reported cash flows support the model’s starting point, but they do not remove the uncertainty around sustained growth, margins, or investment needs.

Valuation

The DCF uses a 10.0% WACC and 2.5% terminal growth13, 38, 39. The Gordon method produces equity value of USD 79,383mm and USD 47.79 per diluted share12, 40. The exit-multiple method, using 21.5x EBITDA, produces equity value of USD 153,989mm and USD 92.71 per diluted share14, 41–43. The terminal values are USD 79,354mm under Gordon and USD 190,106mm under the exit-multiple method; the model flags this gap as outside its tolerance13, 14. We therefore use the Gordon output as the target basis, with the divergence showing how sensitive the DCF is to terminal assumptions.

The relative valuation applies peer medians to AMD’s FY2025 metrics and yields an indicated value of USD 145.52 per share44. The model’s DCF and relative-valuation cross-check delta is negative 20.0%, within the stated mandate tolerance, although the terminal methods remain far apart45. The peer-derived EV/EBITDA range is broad, from 18.74x to 153.83x, which limits the precision of a single multiple-based conclusion44. We retain the Gordon target of USD 47.79 per share and Sell rating12.

Risks

The principal operating risk is that growth remains concentrated in Data Center while other segments contribute less. Data Center represented 63.9% of quarterly revenue; Client and Gaming revenue declined, and All Other’s operating loss widened8, 33, 46. A slowdown in the largest segment would therefore weigh heavily on consolidated results.

Working capital is another watch point. Inventory rose to USD 11,463mm from USD 7,920mm at FY2025, while accounts receivable increased to USD 9,209mm from USD 6,315mm10, 11. If shipments or customer collections weaken, these balances could constrain cash conversion. Finally, the terminal-value spread between valuation methods makes the target sensitive to assumptions about long-run growth and exit value; the Gordon DCF is substantially below the exit-multiple result13, 14.
