## Summary

Operating leverage turned decisively in the September quarter. Revenue of 16,513 [EV:income_statement.revenue] grew 20.9% year over year [EV:derived.revenue_yoy], gross margin reached 43.0% [EV:derived.gross_margin], and operating income of 2,522 [EV:income_statement.operating_income] compares with 683 [EV:income_statement.operating_income] a year earlier. Diluted EPS was 0.60 [EV:income_statement.diluted_eps].

The nine-month result is not the quarter's. A net non-operating charge of 12,184 [EV:income_statement.interest_expense] took pre-tax income to a loss of 10,783 [EV:income_statement.income_before_tax] and net income to a loss of 11,441 [EV:income_statement.net_income].

Thesis delta: the recovery is concentrated in Client Computing and Data Center, and it is not yet a cash recovery — first-nine-month free cash flow was 2,968 [EV:derived.fcf] against capital expenditure of 10,216 [EV:cash_flow.capital_expenditure].

Valuation stance: our DCF yields 23.20 per diluted share on a Gordon terminal value [EV:dcf.per_diluted_share.gordon] and 33.56 on an exit multiple [EV:dcf.per_diluted_share.exit_multiple]; the own-band EV/EBITDA range implies 16.83 to 26.49 [EV:comps.own_band]. The mandate carries no market price, so no upside and no cross-check delta exist.

## Outlook

The forecast is built from a single annual base: revenue of 52,853 [EV:dcf.base.revenue] and an EBITDA margin of 16.16% [EV:dcf.base.ebitda_margin], both FY2025. The gross-margin glide starts at the Q3 FY2026 rate and adds one point a year, inside the mandate band. Blended revenue growth is -0.9% in the first forecast year [EV:dcf.blended_growth] and 3.3% in the final year [EV:dcf.blended_growth].

Three segment paths carry the blend. Client Computing is set at -4% in the first forecast year off its first-nine-month base [EV:segments.ccg.revenue], gliding to +2% [EV:segments.ccg.revenue]. Data Center and AI is set at +1% [EV:segments.dcai.revenue], gliding to +4% [EV:segments.dcai.revenue]. Intel Foundry is set at +3% [EV:segments.foundry.revenue], gliding to +5% [EV:segments.foundry.revenue]. NEX, Altera and Mobileye carry explicit paths at their mandate floors, but the ledger holds no revenue for them, so they contribute no weight to the blend. All Other has revenue of 2,989 [EV:segments.all_other_revenue] but no growth path in the proposal and is excluded from the blend. The forecast is therefore driven by Client Computing, Data Center and Foundry; the other four lines are presentational.

Two divergences must be stated. First, revenue: the base of 52,853 [EV:dcf.base.revenue] is the FY2025 actual, while the first nine months of FY2026 already reached 46,218 [EV:income_statement.revenue], so the first forecast year sits below the current run-rate. Second, free cash flow: the base of 3,957 [EV:dcf.base.fcf] at a 6.42% margin [EV:dcf.base.fcf_margin] is the first-nine-month FY2026 figure annualised, and the forecast holds the EBITDA-to-FCF gap constant rather than re-basing to that run-rate, so the FCF margin rises only as the EBITDA margin rises. The forecast FCF path is a margin-expansion path, not a run-rate path.

The exit-multiple terminal value at 14.0x EBITDA is 230,395.29 [EV:dcf.terminal.exit_multiple.terminal_value], giving equity value of 178,093.03 [EV:dcf.equity_value.exit_multiple] and 33.56 per share [EV:dcf.per_diluted_share.exit_multiple]. Net debt of -3,871 [EV:dcf.net_debt] is added to both.

Relative valuation: the own-band EV/EBITDA range of 10.0x to 16.0x implies 16.83 to 26.49 [EV:comps.own_band]; the P/S range of 2.5x to 7.5x implies 24.90 to 74.69 [EV:comps.own_band]; the P/E range of 12.0x to 26.0x implies -0.60 to -1.31 [EV:comps.own_band] and is not meaningful on a loss-making base.

Cross-check: the mandate sets no market price and the peer block is empty, so neither a DCF upside nor a comps upside can be computed and the cross-check delta is null. The own-band implied prices are reference only; they are not a cross-check.

Terminal-method divergence: the Gordon output of 23.20 [EV:dcf.per_diluted_share.gordon] sits inside the own-band EV/EBITDA range, while the exit-multiple output of 33.56 [EV:dcf.per_diluted_share.exit_multiple] sits above it. The gap is driven by the constant EBITDA-to-FCF gap that holds the FCF path and therefore the Gordon terminal value; we record it rather than reconcile it.
